// 02 — about me

Still early, already building.

I'm a Bachelor of Information Technology student at Bagmati University, Janamaitri Campus, working through the fundamentals — discrete math, data structures, core CS theory — while spending most of my free time actually shipping things.

Right now that means Karigar, a freelance marketplace app built for Nepal: a React frontend, an Express.js backend with JWT auth, a Postgres schema, and a Khalti payment sandbox integration. It's a real attempt at solving a real problem, not just a class project.

Outside of coursework, I'm pulled toward AI and cybersecurity — understanding how systems break is as interesting to me as building the systems in the first place. I'm also actively building toward freelancing as a way to turn what I'm learning into real client work.
